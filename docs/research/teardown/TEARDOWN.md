# Competitive Teardown — Audio & Voice Apps (2024–2026)

Scope: 16 apps across karaoke/duet, DAW/sampler, AI song generation, voice-social, streaming, and hardware. Evidence is App Store/Google Play listing screenshots and official sites in `docs/research/teardown/<app>/`, supplemented by web research for facts not visible on screen (cited inline). Airchat has no discoverable App Store listing under its own name (invite-only, name collision with unrelated apps); its screenshot is a dated press teardown instead. Two previously empty folders (Airchat, Voice Memos) are now filled.

## Per-app notes

**Smule** — Tab nav (Sing/Explore/Duet). Record: pick song → pick key/backing → tap red record → sing over lyric prompter → post (3 taps to sound). Duet is split-screen video/audio against a "top artists" grid. Feedback via hearts + VIP gifting economy + leaderboards. Monetization: VIP subscription gates most of the 15M-song catalog; many songs are pay-per-unlock. Feels premium in its pitch-correction and lyric prompter; dated in cluttered explore/live-battle screens and paywalls that interrupt mid-song. **Steal:** lyric-synced record screen. **Avoid:** per-song paywall walls before you can sing.

**StarMaker** — Bottom tabs Home/Songbook/Studio/Learn/Profile. Record: browse 14M-song songbook → tap song → sing with backing track → duet invite → post. Coins/diamonds gifting drives a live-room economy. Premium: huge catalog + "learn to sing" framing; dated: saturated magenta/purple gradients and gifting UI read as pay-to-win. **Steal:** "Learn to Sing" framing as retention hook. **Avoid:** gift-spam visual language in live rooms.

**BandLab** — Rare hybrid: a social feed (Following/Trending/Music, Twitter-style track posts with waveform thumbnails) fused with a full multitrack DAW (color-coded stem lanes — vocals teal, drums red, bass orange). Record: tap + → pick Sampler/Recorder/instrument → tap record circle → layer takes, auto-cloud-saved. Real-time multitrack collab via "Join With." Notably **free with no paywall** — monetizes via hardware/sample marketplace, not subscription. Feels premium as "GitHub for music"; the dense mixer can overwhelm first-time users. **Steal:** social feed + DAW in one app, free-forever positioning. **Avoid:** mixer information density on first open.

**Voloco** — Studio nav. Record: pick a preset/beat tile (tagged "World Wide," "Triple Double," etc.) → tap the big red record button, **no headphones required** → live auto-tune while singing → share arrow. Waveform: stacked teal-on-black track lanes. Monetization: Pro subscription unlocks premium AI voices/presets. Premium: real-time auto-tune is genuinely magical; dated: preset browsing via a wall of text-chip tags is a weak discovery pattern. **Steal:** headphone-free instant auto-tune as the core hook. **Avoid:** tag-chip preset browsing.

**Rapchat** — Home/Beats/Studio/Profile tabs. Record: browse beat grid (artist-portrait "Type Beat" cards, à la Spotify's genre browsing) → open studio (loop zone, colored waveform tracks, FX row) → record → vocal-tune sliders (Retune Speed, Note Stickiness) → post to a public profile feed. Monetization: Pro unlocks unlimited beats + advanced tune. Feels authentic to rap-community discovery habits ("Travis Scott Type Beat"); the portrait-driven cards verge on generic stock photography. **Steal:** "type beat" discovery framing, exposed vocal-tune parameters. **Avoid:** stock-portrait beat cards.

**Moises** — No feed; a flat "My Recording" library. Flow: import/record a track → AI separates stems in seconds → per-instrument mute/solo sliders (not a classic waveform — horizontal stem bars) → optional synced scrolling chord chart → smart metronome (subdivision, L/R volume, 0.5×/1×/2×). Cinematic real-musician photography in marketing. Monetization is confusing: multiple third-party sources report different tiers ($3.99–$29.99/mo) gating AI Match/unlimited separations [saasworthy.com, thetoolstrunk.com, Oct 2025]. Premium: genuinely useful chord-chart-while-you-play utility; dated: pricing opacity. **Steal:** synced chord chart over playback. **Avoid:** tier/pricing confusion.

**Suno** — Create/Explore/Library tabs. Record flow is really a *compose* flow: type a text prompt (with genre chips: japanese/house/experimental) → tap Create → song renders in ~30–60s with AI cover art, or upload/record reference audio to remix. Free tier gives 50 watermarked credits/day; Pro is $10/mo for 2,500 credits (~500 songs) with commercial rights; Premier is $30/mo for 10,000 credits plus the Suno Studio DAW [Suno pricing pages, 2025]. Waveform is thin and decorative, disconnected from generation. Premium: gradient-matched visual system, prompt-to-song feels magical; dated: no felt connection to "playing" anything. **Steal:** genre-chip prompt refinement. **Avoid:** decorative, non-functional waveform.

**TikTok** — App Store creative is mostly generic content marketing (filters, a black-hole video, a recipe video) rather than record/duet UI. Known model: single vertical full-screen feed (For You/Following), center-+ camera with duet/stitch offered post-capture, action rail (like/comment/share/bookmark) on the right edge, caption+sound credit bottom-left. Monetization via LIVE gifting and Shop, not a paywall. Premium: zero-chrome immersion is still the category benchmark; the App Store listing itself is a missed opportunity to sell the actual record/duet flow.

**Instagram Notes / Threads** — Threads nav is a near-1:1 X clone (Home/Search/Post/Activity/Profile). Notes is a status bubble above the DM inbox: 60-character text cap, or a 30-second music clip / 2-second looping video instead of text [Hootsuite, SetSmart, 2026]. Card anatomy: avatar + handle + timestamp + text/media + like/reply/repost/share row. Monetization: none in-app (ad-supported at the Meta level). Premium: the 60-char cap forces charm and keeps friction near zero; dated: Threads' UI has almost no visual differentiation from X. **Steal:** ultra-low-friction ambient status via a hard character cap. **Avoid:** derivative visual identity.

**Airchat** — No live App Store listing found; captured via a dated third-party teardown screenshot [Alex Debecker, "Product teardown: Airchat," Apr 18 2024, alexdebecker.substack.com]. Dark UI, top tabs "Following / Airchat Addicts," TikTok-style continuous auto-play feed of voice posts with live auto-generated transcripts under each speaker, bottom nav with a persistent center **mic FAB** and an Audio/Video mode toggle. Record flow reported directly by press: posts autoplay and transcribe as you scroll → tap-and-hold the mic → release to post instantly to followers; replies work the same way [TechCrunch, Apr 13 2024] — effectively 1 tap to first sound once the app is open. No monetization at launch (invite-only growth phase). Premium: talk-first authenticity and instant reply loop; risk: invite-gating and transcription-accuracy limit scale. **Steal:** persistent mic FAB across all of nav, live transcription as accessibility *and* skimmability. **Avoid:** invite-only gating as a growth lever if broad reach matters.

**Spotify** — Bottom tabs Home/Search/Library/Premium. Home is shelf-based ("Jump back in") across All/Music/Podcasts/Audiobooks tabs; other screens show real-time lyric-line highlighting and a collaborative-playlist "Blend" (Venn-diagram avatars). 2024–25 brand refresh trades green-heavy chrome for black cards with hot-pink/neon-green blocking and huge condensed headline type. Monetization is a hard Premium paywall (ads on free); the new Premium Platinum tier (2025) bundles Lossless audio (up to 24-bit/44.1kHz FLAC) with the AI DJ and DJ-software integrations [Spotify Newsroom, Sept 10 2025]. This is the cleanest, most confident typographic/color system in the set — nothing reads dated. **Steal:** condensed oversized headline type + saturated color-blocking; bundling a premium *feature* (Lossless) with a premium *feeling* (AI DJ) in one tier.

**SoundCloud** — Home/Search/Library/Upload/Profile tabs. Signature pattern: a full-width scrubbable **waveform with timestamped comments pinned at the exact second** they were left, plus an emoji-reaction bar. 2025 updated its business model to 0% distribution fee (100% royalties to artists), "Fan-Powered Royalties" (payout scales with a fan's own listening, not a pooled pro-rata split), and a zero-commission fan-to-artist tip button [SoundCloud Help Center; Digital Music News, Oct 30–31 2025]. Premium: the timestamped-waveform comment is arguably the single best audio-social interaction pattern reviewed; dated: overall chrome hasn't visibly evolved since ~2015. **Steal:** comments anchored to waveform position. **Avoid:** stale icon-row visual system.

**Voice Memos (Apple)** — No tabs, a flat "All Recordings" list with one red record circle pinned bottom-center — **1 tap to first sound** from cold open. Recording screen shows a large centered live waveform (thick red = recorded, thin white = remaining) with a blue playhead and timer. Free, no monetization, no social loop at all. iOS 26 added Studio Voice / Spatial Audio recording [Apple, 2025]. This is the cleanest, most legible waveform and the fastest record flow of any app reviewed. **Steal:** the entire "one button, instant waveform, no setup" pattern. **Avoid:** nothing to avoid — it's the utility baseline every record button should be measured against.

**Endel** — Captured from the official site (no app-store listing shown): near-black brand system, "Personalized soundscapes... backed by neuroscience," mode-first nav (Focus/Sleep/Relax/Activity) shown consistently across iPhone, Apple Watch, CarPlay and Vision Pro mockups, press logos (WSJ, Rolling Stone, Wired, Pitchfork, Guardian). There's no "record" — it's generative: pick a mode, audio starts, adjust a small dial set. Visualization is an abstract mirrored-line "orb," not a literal waveform. Subscription-only, no free tier shown. This is the calmest, most disciplined "premium wellness" visual language of any app in the set — pure black/white, one accent glow. **Steal:** cross-device consistency (phone/watch/car/headset shown as one system) and choosing *not* to use a waveform when the product isn't about a discrete recording.

**Koala Sampler** — No navigation hierarchy at all: a single screen with a Sample/Sequence/Perform tab switcher. Screens show waveform-tile sample pads with Vol/Pitch/Fan knobs and One Shot/Reverse/Loop chips, a step sequencer with BPM/piano-roll, playfully named FX presets ("Vanilla," "Strawberry"), and a 16-pad performance grid colored by sample type. $4.99 one-time purchase plus optional ~$2.99 sample packs — no subscription [App Store; equipboard.com]. Colorful, tactile, toy-like without being childish. Nothing dated — this is one of the tightest single-screen music-tool UIs reviewed. **Steal:** the three-mode (Sample/Sequence/Perform) mental model as the *entire* IA. **Avoid:** n/a.

**OP-1 field (Teenage Engineering)** — Not an app; official product site uses monochrome grey line-art renders, bilingual (EN/JP) nav copy, and icon-only hardware labels (a red dot for record, scissors for punch-in, a loop glyph) instead of text — "instrument," not "app." In a 2025 "Flipped Out" stunt, TE let buyers pay anywhere from $1,399 to $9,999 for the same hardware [MusicRadar, MusicTech, June 2025]. One accent color (red) against all-grey chrome. Nothing dated — TE's whole brand exists to avoid ever looking like default tech. **Steal:** icon-only controls and a single disciplined accent color. **Avoid:** n/a (hardware pricing stunt doesn't transfer to a mobile app).

## Cross-app synthesis

**Anatomy of a great record button.** The best examples (Voice Memos, Airchat, Voloco, Koala) agree on a formula: one unmistakable red circle, bottom-safe-area, zero pre-record modal or setup screen, and the waveform must start drawing the instant the tap registers — feedback latency is the single biggest "does this feel cheap" signal. Secondary transport (play/pause/skip) is always visually smaller and greyer so record never has to compete for attention. Airchat and BandLab go further by making the mic a *persistent* nav-bar element rather than a screen-specific control, which shortens the path from "open app" to "make sound" across the whole product, not just one screen.

**Best waveform treatments, ranked.** (1) SoundCloud's timestamped-comment waveform — turns amplitude into a social surface. (2) Voice Memos' live dual-tone (red recorded / white remaining) with a blue playhead — the most legible, zero-ambiguity utility waveform. (3) BandLab/Rapchat's color-per-stem multitrack lanes — best for signaling "this is a real mix," not a single clip. (4) Endel's abstract mirrored-line orb — the strongest argument for *replacing* the waveform metaphor entirely when the product is generative rather than recorded. Weakest: Suno's thin decorative bars, which don't represent anything the user actually did.

**Onboarding ranked by taps-to-value** (best first): Voice Memos (1 tap, sound already possible) → Koala Sampler (1–2 taps, single screen, pads visible on open) → Airchat (~4 steps: invite → name/photo → follow a few → land in an already-playing feed, but fast and audio starts autoplaying immediately) → Voloco/Rapchat (2–3 taps but require browsing a preset/beat first) → BandLab (3–4 taps; feed-first design means finding "+" isn't obvious) → Suno (must compose a text prompt before any sound exists at all — a *compose* flow wearing a *record* app's clothing) → Smule/StarMaker (large song catalogs plus frequent paywalls before you can sing — the most friction of the set) → Moises (no capture-first path at all; you must already have or select audio before anything happens).

**Table stakes vs. differentiators (2026).**

| Table stakes (expected by users) | Differentiators (still a moat) |
|---|---|
| Dark-mode-first recording UI | Real-time collaborative multitrack (BandLab) |
| One-tap record, live waveform feedback | Timestamped social comments anchored to waveform (SoundCloud) |
| AI noise reduction / vocal tuning | Per-listener "fan-powered" royalty payouts (SoundCloud) |
| Lyric/transcript sync during playback | Lossless audio bundled with an AI co-host (Spotify Platinum) |
| Cloud auto-save, no explicit "save" step | Voice-only feed with live auto-transcription (Airchat) |
| One-tap share to social/export | Generative, record-free ambient audio (Endel) |
| Freemium tier with a hard subscription wall | Pay-what-you-want retail as a marketing stunt (Teenage Engineering) |

## Top 10 takeaways for AKINTI

1. Copy Voice Memos' physics: one red circle, no modal, waveform draws the instant you tap — this is the ceiling for record-flow speed.
2. Make the mic a persistent nav-bar element (Airchat/BandLab pattern), not a screen-specific control, so "make sound" is always one tap away.
3. Steal SoundCloud's timestamped-waveform comment — it is the best unclaimed audio-social interaction pattern among direct competitors.
4. Adopt Spotify's typographic confidence (oversized condensed headlines, saturated color-blocking, generous negative space) as the bar for "feels expensive."
5. BandLab proves a free-forever social+DAW hybrid can win Editors' Choice — a subscription wall is not mandatory for a premium feel.
6. Avoid Smule/StarMaker's pattern of gating the *core action* (singing a specific song) behind a paywall — that reads as bait-and-switch.
7. Avoid Moises' multi-tier pricing opacity; pick one clear pricing story and state it on the App Store page itself.
8. Endel shows cross-device consistency (phone/watch/car/headset shown as one coherent system) is itself a premium signal worth designing for early.
9. Suno shows that a compose-first flow (type before you hear anything) can still feel magical if the *output* (art, genre chips) carries enough visual craft — but don't fake a waveform that isn't functionally connected to anything.
10. Koala Sampler's three-mode IA (Sample/Sequence/Perform) is proof that a music tool needs zero navigation hierarchy if the modes themselves are the map — worth testing against AKINTI's own information architecture.
